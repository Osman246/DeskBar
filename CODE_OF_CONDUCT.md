# Code of conduct

DeskBar is a small project and I'd like it to stay a friendly one. Whether you're opening an issue,
sending a pull request or joining a discussion, please be kind to the people you're talking to.

A few things that help:

- Assume good intent. Everyone here is giving up their own time.
- Disagree with the idea or the code, not the person. If something's wrong, say what would fix it.
- Be patient with people who are new. A first issue deserves a helpful answer.
- If I say no to a feature, it's usually because DeskBar is meant to stay small, not because the
  idea is bad.

What isn't OK: harassment, insults, discrimination of any kind, sharing someone's private details,
unwanted sexual attention, or spamming and trolling.

If someone makes you uncomfortable, let me know. Click the **···** menu on their comment, issue or
pull request, choose **Report content**, then **Report to repository admins**. It comes straight to
me and nobody else sees it. I'll look at it within a week.

If it's needed, I'll hide the comment, warn the person or block them from the project. For anything
serious, I'll block them straight away.

# Code of conduct

DeskBar is a small project, and everyone who takes part in it (in issues, pull requests,
discussions and reviews) should be able to do so without being made to feel unwelcome.

## What's expected

- Be kind and assume good intent. People here are giving their time for free.
- Criticise ideas and code, not people. Say what's wrong and, where you can, how to fix it.
- Keep it on topic, and respect a "no" to a feature: DeskBar is small on purpose.
- Welcome newcomers. A first issue or first pull request deserves a patient answer.

## What isn't accepted

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks, in public or private.
- Discrimination or slurs of any kind, including about gender, sexuality, disability, ethnicity,
  religion, age or appearance.
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sexual content or unwanted attention.
- Spam, trolling, or repeatedly derailing a conversation.

## Reporting

If someone breaks this code, open the menu (**···**) on their comment, issue or pull request, choose
**Report content**, then **Report to repository admins**. The report goes privately to the
maintainer. Reports are read within a week and kept confidential.

## What happens

Depending on how serious it is, the maintainer may hide or edit the content, give a warning, or
block someone from the project. A serious or repeated breach can mean a permanent block straight
away.

This code applies everywhere in this repository, and to anyone representing the project elsewhere.
